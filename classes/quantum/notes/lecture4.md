## stabilizer tableau 


- a stabilizer table


$[z | x | r]$

- there are 3 components:
    - is the matrix stabilized by Z?
    - is the matrix stabilized by X?
    - is the phase

- one means the matrix is stabilized by the matrix, 0 mean it isn't.

- we can express $\ket{0}$ as $\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
    - $\ket{0}$ is stabilized by Z.
    - $\ket{0}$ is not stabilized by X.
    - $\ket{0}$'s phase is 0
